Outfit Canvas

Click items from the right sidebar to add them. Drag to position.

💡 Tip: Drag items to arrange them. Click the background of an item to bring it forward.

How to Build an Outfit

  1. Click on items from the sidebar to add them to the canvas
  2. Drag items around to position them
  3. Layer items by dragging - items on top have higher z-index
  4. Click "Save Outfit" when you're happy with the arrangement
