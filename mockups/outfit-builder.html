Outfit Canvas

Click items from the right sidebar to add them. Drag to position.

💡 Tip: Drag items to arrange them. Click the background of an item to bring it forward.

How to Build an Outfit

  1. 📸 Optional: Upload a body/mannequin photo as background
  2. Click on items from the sidebar to add them to the canvas
  3. Drag items around to position them on the body
  4. Layer items by clicking - last clicked item moves to top
  5. Use "Remove Background" button to clear the body image
  6. Click "Save Outfit" when you're happy with the arrangement
